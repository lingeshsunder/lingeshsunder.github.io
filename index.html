About

Where deep network expertise meets cloud and AI.

I'm Lingeshwaran S. — a Cloud Network Architect based in Bengaluru with 14 years designing, deploying, and evolving enterprise-grade network and cloud infrastructure for global clients at Ericsson India Global Services.

My expertise sits at the intersection of SD-WAN, SASE, Cloud Architecture, and network automation. I've seen this industry shift from hardware-centric MPLS to software-defined everything, and I've been hands-on at every stage — not just advising, but building.

"Technology without business impact is just cost. Every architecture decision I make is anchored in one question: does this deliver measurable value?"

Beyond client work, I build my own tools — from Netshape, a production WAN emulator adopted by my team, to Vaultes, an AI/ML cloud cost optimisation tool I'm developing independently.
